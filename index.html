    FAQ

    Questions,
    answered plainly

    What services do you offer?

    Road construction, civil works, township infrastructure, earthworks and property development, delivered on time and within budget.

    How long have you been building?

    Gateway Construction has proudly served Zimbabwe since 1992, building a strong reputation over three decades for our commitment to excellence in construction.

    What is your commitment to quality?

    We adhere to ZWS ISO 9001:2015 standards and are dedicated to consistent, quality infrastructure. We focus on exceeding customer expectations through a culture of “doing it right the first time”.

    How do you manage time and budget?

    We prioritise project management and planning, so timelines and budgets are well defined and strictly followed. Our track record speaks for our efficiency and reliability.
