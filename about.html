Our story

A Zimbabwean builder
with a long view

Founded in 1992, Gateway has been at the forefront of Zimbabwe’s civil engineering sector, building roads, homes, hospitals, schools and factories.

We work closely with local stakeholders, so every project genuinely benefits the people who use it.

  • Roads, civil works and townships
  • Homes, schools, clinics, factories
  • ZWS ISO 9001:2015 certified
